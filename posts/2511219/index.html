This article introduces the compilation process of Qemu, including the preparation of the compilation environment, the compilation method for the aarch64 architecture, optionally enabling O0 and -g3 debugging information, the steps to compile glib and qemu, and the operation of automatically generating compile_commands.json during compilation. Finally, it explains the method of using .gdbinit in the QEMU source directory for gdb debugging.
